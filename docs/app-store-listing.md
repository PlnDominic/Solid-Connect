## Description [4000]
Solid Connect helps you find a trusted local service provider in Ghana, without relying on a friend's phone number or an unverified listing.

POST A JOB IN MINUTES
Describe what you need, add photos, pick your neighbourhood and set a budget. You can edit your post while it is open. Location sharing is optional: you can always choose your area by hand.

COMPARE QUOTES OR LET PROVIDERS COME TO YOU
Send a request to providers near you and compare their quotes, or put a job on the job board and let interested providers respond. Browse providers on the map, save your favourites and see ratings and past work before you decide.

CHAT BEFORE YOU HIRE
Ask questions, share photos and send voice notes with a provider before you accept anything. Hire only when you are happy with the answers.

VERIFIED PROVIDERS, REAL REPUTATION
Providers submit identity documents for verification and build a profile with a portfolio, ratings and job history that stay with them. You can see who you are hiring.

FOLLOW THE JOB FROM START TO FINISH
See when work starts and when it is finished. Confirm completion, then rate your provider. If something goes wrong, open a dispute from the job.

YOU PAY YOUR PROVIDER DIRECTLY
Solid Connect never holds your money and takes no commission on jobs. You agree the price with your provider and settle it between you.

SAFETY BUILT IN
Report or block any user at any time. Your location is shared with your provider or customer only while a job is active. Lock the app with Face ID if you like.

FOR PROVIDERS
Switch your account to a provider profile, then follow the setup checklist: choose your services, set your hours and the areas you cover, add a portfolio and get verified. Receive requests that match your skills, send quotes and build a track record. Your first job is free, then a yearly fee applies. There is no commission on the work you win.

FOR BUSINESSES
Create an organisation, add team members and manage recurring or multi-job projects in one place.

INVITE FRIENDS
Share your referral code with people who need a pro or want to join as one.
