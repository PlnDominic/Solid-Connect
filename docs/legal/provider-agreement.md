# Solid Connect — Provider Agreement

**DRAFT — not reviewed by counsel. See `README.md` in this folder before
using this document for anything.**

_Last drafted: 2026-09-13._

This agreement supplements the general `terms-of-service.md` with terms
specific to using Solid Connect as a provider. Where the two conflict, this
document governs for provider-specific matters.

## 1. You are an independent contractor

**Nothing in this agreement or your use of the App makes you an employee,
agent, partner, or joint venturer of Solid Connect.** You are an
independent business providing services directly to customers you're
matched with. You are responsible for your own:

- Taxes and any business registration the law requires for your trade.
- Tools, materials, and insurance for the work you perform.
- Compliance with any professional licensing your trade requires (see
  Section 4).

Solid Connect does not direct how you perform a job, set your working
hours, or provide you with equipment.

## 2. Getting verified

To appear as a verified provider, you submit identity and (depending on the
verification level you're pursuing) professional documents through the
App. Solid Connect's admin team reviews these and approves or rejects each
submission, with a reason given if rejected. You may resubmit after
addressing the reason. See `terms-of-service.md` Section 4 for the level
structure.

**Submitting false or altered documents is grounds for immediate account
suspension** and may be reported to relevant authorities where the law
requires it.

## 3. Categories, service areas, and availability

You choose which service categories you offer, which neighborhoods/areas
you serve, and your availability status (available now, scheduled,
unavailable, or paused). These directly control which job requests you're
matched against — keep them accurate so you're matched with work you
actually want and can do.

## 4. Professional licensing is your responsibility

Solid Connect's verification confirms what you submitted to us; **it does
not certify that you hold every license your trade legally requires**
(electrical work, for example, often has statutory licensing
requirements in Ghana independent of anything Solid Connect checks). You
are responsible for holding any license the law requires for the work you
accept, and for being able to show it to a customer who asks.

## 5. Quoting and accepting jobs

- You may quote on a customer's posted request, or accept a request sent
  directly to you.
- A quote you submit is an offer at that price; once a customer accepts it,
  you are agreeing to perform the described job at that price, to be paid
  directly by the customer when the work is done (see Section 6).
- If you can no longer do a job you've been matched to, communicate that to
  the customer through the App as early as possible rather than going
  silent — repeated no-shows or unresponsiveness are conduct issues under
  Section 8.

## 6. Getting paid and your subscription

- **You are paid directly by the customer**, in cash or Mobile Money, when
  the work is done. Solid Connect does not collect, hold or pay out money for
  jobs and takes no commission on them.
- Agree the price and how you will be paid with the customer before you
  start. Solid Connect cannot recover an unpaid job for you; if a customer
  does not pay, report it to support and it may count against that
  customer's standing.
- **Subscription.** Your first accepted job is free. After that, quoting and
  accepting direct requests needs an active yearly subscription, paid to
  Solid Connect through the App. A subscription buys a fixed period and
  renewing adds a new period on top. It is the only payment taken through the
  App.

## 7. Reviews and your rating

Customers rate completed jobs 1–5 stars with an optional comment. Your
displayed rating is the average of all your non-removed reviews. Solid
Connect may remove a review that violates the review rules in
`terms-of-service.md` Section 8, which recalculates your rating
accordingly — removing a bad-faith review is not something you need to
dispute separately.

## 8. Conduct specific to providers

In addition to the general conduct rules in `terms-of-service.md` Section
10, as a provider you agree not to:

- Accept a job you don't intend to complete, or accept work outside a
  license you're required to hold.
- Take a job that was found through the App off the App to avoid its
  dispute and verification protections.
- Misrepresent your trade, experience, or verification status.

Violating this section can result in suspension. A suspended provider
account cannot accept new job requests or submit new quotes; the app
enforces this the next time you sign in (not instantly mid-session — see
the note on suspension enforcement in `docs/roadmap-and-risks.md`).

## 9. Disputes involving your work

If a customer disputes a job you performed, Solid Connect's admin team
reviews the evidence (job timeline, chat, any photos) and may resolve it
and record an outcome (it cannot move money, since Solid Connect does not
hold any). See `refund-dispute-policy.md`
for the customer-facing version of this process. You will be able to see
the dispute's outcome; if you disagree with a resolution, contact
**support@solidconnect.co**.

## 10. Ending this agreement

You may stop using the App at any time. Solid Connect may suspend or
terminate your provider access for a Terms or Provider Agreement
violation, with the reason given to you in the App.
