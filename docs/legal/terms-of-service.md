## 2. What Solid Connect is

Solid Connect is a marketplace app that connects **customers** in Accra,
Ghana who need a home-services job done (plumbing, electrical, carpentry,
cleaning, and similar categories) with **providers** — plumbers,
electricians, artisans, and cleaners — who offer those services.

**Solid Connect is a facilitator, not the employer of providers and not the
performer of any service.** We provide the app, the matching, the chat, the
escrow/payment flow, and the review system. The actual work is a contract
directly between the customer and the provider. We are not a party to that
work contract and do not guarantee its outcome, though we do provide the
verification, dispute, and refund mechanisms described below to make that
trust real rather than assumed.

One account can hold both a customer role and a provider role; you can
switch between them at any time in the app.

## 3. Eligibility and accounts

- You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

  all depend on this being correct.
- You are responsible for anything that happens under your account. Tell us
  immediately (see Section 11) if you believe your account has been
  compromised.
- One person may not hold more than one account. We may merge, suspend, or
  remove accounts that violate this.

## 4. Provider verification

Providers on Solid Connect go through a staged verification process before
they can carry certain trust marks in the app:

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| Registered | Signed up, no documents reviewed yet |
| Identity verified | Government-issued ID reviewed and approved by Solid Connect |
| Profession verified | Trade credentials/experience reviewed and approved |
| Experience verified | A track record of completed jobs on the platform |
| Solid Connect Verified | The highest mark — all of the above |

Verification is performed by Solid Connect's admin team based on documents
a provider submits. **Verification is not a guarantee of the provider's
work quality or a certification of professional competence** — for
regulated trades, you should independently confirm any license the law
requires (see Section 9). Verification can be revoked if we later find the
submitted information was false or the provider's conduct warrants it.

## 5. Posting a job and getting matched

- Customers describe the job, its category, and location; Solid Connect
  matches nearby, relevant providers using the location and category you
  provide.
- A provider who responds gives you a quote. You choose whether to accept
  it. Accepting a quote creates a job and a payment held by Solid Connect
  (see Section 7) — it does **not** create an employment relationship
  between you and the provider, or between the provider and Solid Connect.
- Once a job is accepted, in-app chat, progress tracking, and (for the
  duration the job is active) each party's live location become available
  to the other party, to make coordinating the job easier and more
  accountable. Location sharing is foreground-only, requires your
  permission, and is deleted the moment the job completes — see
  `privacy-policy.md`.

## 6. Job completion and confirmation

- The provider marks the job as done when they've finished the work.
- The customer then confirms completion in the app. Confirming releases the
  held payment (see Section 7) and unlocks the ability to leave a review.
- If something is wrong with the completed work, do not confirm — open a
  dispute instead (see `refund-dispute-policy.md`).

## 7. Payments and commission

- When a customer accepts a quote, the agreed price is held by Solid
  Connect on the customer's behalf until the job is confirmed complete.
- On confirmation, Solid Connect releases that amount to the provider,
  minus a commission (the rate shown in the app at the time — see
  `docs/legal/README.md` for why this isn't a fixed number here).
- **As of this draft, Solid Connect does not yet process real payments.**
  Card and mobile money charging is being built; until it is live, any
  in-app payment status is for tracking purposes only and no real funds
  move through the app. This section describes the intended mechanics once
  real payment processing is connected, not the current state.

## 8. Reviews

After a job completes, the customer may leave a 1–5 star rating and an
optional written comment on the provider. Reviews are meant to reflect a
genuine, completed job — do not post a review for a job that didn't happen
through the app, and do not offer or accept payment in exchange for a
review. Solid Connect may remove a review that violates this or that is
abusive, and may adjust a provider's aggregate rating accordingly.

## 9. Professional licensing and your own diligence

Some trades (electrical work in particular) may be subject to licensing or
certification requirements under Ghanaian law independent of anything Solid
Connect verifies. **Solid Connect's verification levels are not a
substitute for confirming a provider holds any license the law requires for
their trade.** Customers engaging providers for regulated work should ask
to see that license directly.
